The first release of Radietto - a Pandora-style radio app powered by LLMs. Core features:
- Genre taste sliders for personalization
- Pre-generated radio stations with fun names, taglines, and emojis
- LLM-powered song selection via OpenRouter
- YouTube audio streaming with background playback
- Thumbs up/down/skip feedback loop
- Custom prompt-based station creation
- Lazy pre-fetching and queue management
